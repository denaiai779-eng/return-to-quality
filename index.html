How It Works
  1. Pick the situation that matches the game (RISP, 2 Outs, etc.)
  2. Tap RELEASE THE MAKO → spin reveals a challenge
  3. Read it before his AB / cage rep
  4. If he does it → tap ✓ DID IT (points bank)
  5. If he had the chance and didn't → tap ✗ DIDN'T (−1 Bronze/Silver, −2 Gold/Diamond)
  6. If it never came up — walk, inning ended — tap N/A (no change)
  7. For lazy ABs / bad behavior → tap − TAKE AWAY and pick the reason (points deducted)
  8. Stack points toward weekly + season rewards. Weekly resets Mondays.
